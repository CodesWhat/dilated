Dilated 1.1.0 can restore your automation rules from the last good backup when they won't load, and remembers holds you stopped so dilated run doesn't restart them.
It asks on the second launch whether to check for updates automatically, ends a download watch whose scan stalls, and removes its Claude Code, Codex and OpenCode hooks when you prepare to uninstall. Closed-lid removal and repair are safer when an older helper script is still running.
Closed-lid mode is still experimental. Its hardware recovery checks were not run for this release.
